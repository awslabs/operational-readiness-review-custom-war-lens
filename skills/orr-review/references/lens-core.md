How to score one question:

1. Selected means the statements that are true today and backed by evidence.
2. If None of these (`none_no`) is selected, alone or with other statements, the question scores its maximum risk.
3. Otherwise go through the risk lines in order; the first line that is true gives the risk. A statement id is true when it is selected. AND needs both sides, OR needs at least one side, NOT turns true into false, and parentheses group. "otherwise" is always true.
4. A question marked not applicable as a whole, with a reason, has no risk level: report it as Not applicable. A question not answered yet is Unanswered, never No risk.
